Northvale FellowshipAI Use Policy · v1

How our staff uses AI

We use AI as a helper, never as a replacement for prayer, pastoral care, or real relationships. These guidelines apply to all staff and ministry volunteers.

We will

  • Use AI for first drafts, captions, summaries and scheduling ideas.
  • Have a person review and edit everything before it is published.
  • Tell people when an image or video was made with AI.

We will not

  • Enter names, prayer requests, giving data or anything about minors.
  • Publish testimonies, quotes or photos of people that AI invented.
  • Use AI to write sermons or pastoral counsel.

Who to ask

Questions go to the Communications Director. This policy is reviewed every six months.

Five things AI never does here

  • Answers a prayer request or a pastoral DM
  • Invents a testimony or a quote
  • Sees anyone's personal information
  • Posts without a person's final review
  • Speaks for the pastor

Claude Skills

Skill chains for ministry teams

A Skill is a saved set of instructions, examples and files that Claude follows the same way every time. Chaining Skills means the output of one becomes the input of the next, so one Sunday sermon can become a week of content, with a person signing off at each checkpoint.

Example chain: Sunday to the whole week

Questions

Common questions

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not much. We build your workflows with Claude, so the main addition is a Claude plan for the staff who will use it. Everything else works with tools you likely already have, like Canva and Subsplash. We recommend other paid tools only when they clearly save time.

Why do you build with Claude?

Claude lets us package your church's voice, guardrails and weekly tasks into Skills that work the same way every time and can be shared across your whole team. It is also strong at careful, natural writing, which matters when every word represents your church. If your team already uses a different assistant, we will help you decide what fits.

Will AI replace our communications staff?

No. The goal is to give your people more time for the work only people can do: relationships, storytelling and care. AI handles first drafts and busywork.

Is our congregation going to be uncomfortable with this?

Being open helps. We help you explain how and why your church uses AI, and the policy commits to labeling AI-made images and video.

We are a very small church. Is this for us?

Yes. Small teams often benefit the most, because a few hours saved each week matters more when one person does everything.
