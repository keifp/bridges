Seasonal campaign

Advent

Twenty-five days of posts for December, built on a strict Swiss grid. One verse a day, a reading plan, interactive stories and a Christmas Eve invitation.

Concept project
ClientBrookhollow Church
RunDec 1–25
Deliverables25 posts, stories, plan, invite
ToolsCanva templates, Metricool

The system

One post every day from December 1 to Christmas. Each post is a single verse on a strict five-column grid: the date, a huge number, one line of Scripture. The design stays the same every day, so the changing words are the only thing people notice.
