Sunday media pack

Rooted

A full set of weekly screen media for a six-week series on spiritual roots: pre-roll, a live countdown, video wall loops, lower thirds and announcement slides. Everything shares one look, so the room feels like the series from the moment people walk in.

Concept project · Cedarline Church is a fictional client
ClientCedarline Church
ScreensCenter screen, side screens, LED wall
DeliverablesPre-roll, countdown, loops, slides
ToolsPremiere, Photoshop, ProPresenter

The look

Deep forest green, warm gold and an off-white bone color. Thin gold lines grow like roots across every screen. The type is a classic serif with wide spacing, calm and steady, which fits a series about going deep instead of going fast.

Series pre-roll

A short title open that plays right before the sermon. Gold roots grow out from the center, then the title settles in. This preview loops in your browser; the final file is a 1080p ProRes export with a sound design hit on the title.

Countdown

A five-minute countdown that runs on the center screen before service. It's a working timer, so go ahead and press start. On a Sunday it runs as a ProPresenter timer with the series background behind it.

Service begins in 5:00
"Rooted and built up in him." Colossians 2:7 Countdown · 16:9

Video wall loop

An ultra-wide motion background for the LED wall behind the stage. It moves slowly enough to sit under worship without pulling attention away. Built at 7680 × 2160 and split across the wall's panels.

Faint lines show where the LED panels meet, so we can keep text and key shapes off the seams.

Lower thirds and slides

Speaker names for the livestream and side screens, plus announcement slides in three color options so the weekly slides never blur together.
