Process

How a project runs

1

Listen

We meet with your staff and ministry leaders to learn your voice, your people and what's coming up.

2

Plan

A content calendar, shot lists and a capture schedule, shared in Monday.com so everyone can see it.

3

Make

Shoot, edit and design in Premiere, Lightroom, Photoshop and Canva, then schedule in Metricool.

4

Learn

Monthly reviews of what worked and what didn't, so each month's content is better than the last.

About

Media made by people who love the local church

Erco Media is a small studio run by Keith Pace. We believe a church's online presence should sound like the people in its building, and that the best content comes from paying attention to what is already happening on a Sunday morning.

We work with communications teams of every size, from a single staff member to a full department, and we're happy to plug into the systems you already use.

Have a series, an event, or a feed that needs some life?

Tell us what's coming up and we'll send back a plan.
