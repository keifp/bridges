Wildfire

A summer camp campaign for a student ministry, with a retro look that runs from the first teaser post to the camp shirts.

Concept project
ClientRidgeview Students
AudienceStudents 6–12 and parents
DeliverablesPosts, reel, info card, merch
ToolsPhotoshop, Canva, Premiere

The look

Wildfire borrows from 1970s camp brochures and national park posters: striped sunsets, a chunky rounded serif with a drop shadow, a warm sand background and a forest green. It feels fun and a little nostalgic, which works for students and parents.

Sign-up posts

Four posts that run from February to the early-bird deadline: the reveal, sign-ups open, a countdown and a bring-a-friend push.
