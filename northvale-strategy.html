04What we post

Four content pillars

40%Stories

Real people and what God is doing in their lives. Baptisms, testimonies, volunteers.

25%Teaching

Short sermon clips and carousels that are useful on a Tuesday.

20%Behind the scenes

Set-up, rehearsals, staff, the people behind Sunday.

15%Invites

Events and next steps, kept to one in every six or seven posts.

07The plan

90 days, three phases

Days 1–30 · Set up
  • Refresh bios, highlights and links
  • Recruit and train 4 capture volunteers
  • Start the weekly rhythm
Days 31–60 · Build
  • Launch a testimony reel series
  • Add YouTube Shorts from every sermon
  • Reply to every comment within 24 hours
Days 61–90 · Learn
  • Review what worked in Metricool
  • Drop the weakest format, double the best
  • Plan the next quarter with ministry leads
08The ask

What we need from the team

One 30-minute check-in each month with ministry leaders · Access to Metricool and Meta Business Suite · A pastor on call for prayer requests and sensitive DMs · Permission to post the real, unpolished moments.

Staying current

Platforms change fast. Part of the strategy is a simple monthly habit for keeping up without chasing every trend.

Watch

Fifteen minutes a week on platform news and creator updates from Instagram, Meta and YouTube.

Test

Try one new format a month, like a trending audio or a new carousel style, and give it four posts before judging it.

Filter

Ask one question before using any trend: does this help us tell the truth about our church?
