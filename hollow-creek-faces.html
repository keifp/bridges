Photography and storytelling

Faces of Hollow Creek

A black and white portrait and testimony series. Twelve people, five questions, and one month of posts, plus a printed display wall for the church lobby.

Concept project
ClientHollow Creek Church
PlatformsInstagram, Facebook, lobby
DeliverablesPortraits, carousels, prints
ToolsLightroom, Photoshop

The idea

Every church is full of stories nobody has heard. Over one month we photographed twelve people and asked each of them the same five questions. The look is documentary: black and white, real light, no posing, and plain typewriter-style text so the words feel like a note, not an ad.

Photograph

Twenty-minute portrait sessions in places that matter to each person: their kitchen, their workshop, the pew where they sit.

Listen

A short recorded interview using the same five questions, so the stories can be compared and grouped later.

Share

One story a week on Instagram and Facebook, with the full set printed for a lobby display.

Story posts

Each person gets a three-slide carousel: the portrait with one line, the longer story, and an invitation. Captions stay in their own words, edited only for length.

The five questions

Same questions, every interview. They are open enough that people tell stories instead of giving answers.

  1. How did you first end up at Hollow Creek?
  2. What was going on in your life at the time?
  3. Who is someone here who made a difference for you?
  4. Where do you see God at work right now?
  5. What would you tell someone walking in for the first time?
