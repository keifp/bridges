Social campaign

Unhurried

A four-week social rollout for a sermon series on Sabbath rest. The goal was to reach people in their 20s and 30s who feel worn out, and give them one honest reason to show up on Sunday.

Concept project
ClientLanternfield Church
PlatformsInstagram, Facebook, YouTube
DeliverablesCarousels, reels, stories, calendar
Timeline2 weeks prep · 4-week series

The idea

Most church promo shouts. This one slows down. Every piece uses a classic, sacred look: warm linen paper, an arched window with a sun rising through engraved lines in rust and navy, wide-spaced serif capitals and a thin gold outline. Lots of empty space and short lines of text make the feed itself feel like a deep breath. The voice is plain and a little honest about how tired everyone is.

Who it's for

Young adults and young parents who are busy, burned out, and skeptical of anything that feels like a sales pitch.

What it says

"You weren't made to run on empty." Rest is a gift from God, not a reward you earn.

What success looks like

More saves and shares than likes, first-time guest cards that mention Instagram, and replies that start real conversations.

Caption and posting notes

  • Opens with a question so people answer in the comments.
  • Asks for a save and a send, since those count most with the algorithm right now.
  • One emoji, no hashtag wall. Three hashtags in the first comment.
  • Post Tuesday at 7:30 pm, when this audience is most active.
  • Cross-post to Facebook through Meta Business Suite with a shorter caption.

Comments and DMs

Replying is part of the campaign. Each reply should sound like a real person at the church: warm, short, and never a canned script. Prayer requests and anything sensitive go to a pastor the same day.

Comment on the launch carousel
honestly the last time I rested was 2019 😅
Ha, you're not alone. Want to try step 1 with us this Sunday? Phone in a drawer, one hour. We'll save you a seat ☕
Direct message
Is this series for people who don't really go to church? I'm kind of burned out on church too tbh
That's a really honest question, thank you for asking it. Yes, it's for anyone who's tired, church tired included. Nobody will put you on the spot. Can I tell you what to expect on a Sunday?

What we'd measure

Targets we'd set with the comms team before launch, then check in the monthly review. These are goals for the concept, not real results.

Saves

Target: saves and shares outpace likes on every carousel.

Reach

Target: at least half of reel views come from non-followers.

Visits

Target: guest cards that list Instagram as how they heard.
