Sermon clips and YouTube

Pinecrest

One sermon a week, turned into six short videos and a set of thumbnails people actually click. A repeatable system for a small team.

Concept project
ClientPinecrest Church
PlatformsYouTube, Reels, Facebook
DeliverablesClip workflow, thumbnails, Shorts
ToolsPulpit AI, Premiere, Metricool

The system

Pinecrest records a great sermon every week and then posts it once. This workflow turns each sermon into a full week of short videos without adding hours. Pulpit AI does the first pass; a person always makes the final picks.

Thumbnails

A thumbnail system with three color modes, so the feed looks varied but still belongs together. Short words, a face, one bold highlight. Every thumbnail is tested at phone size before it goes up.

Shorts

Each short is 30 to 60 seconds, reframed to vertical, with bold word-by-word captions since most people watch with the sound off. The first two seconds always start mid-thought.
